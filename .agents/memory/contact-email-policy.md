---
name: Contact email policy
description: User-approved sender and recipient behavior for contact enquiries.
---
Contact submissions should notify the receiving inbox without sending an automatic acknowledgment to the customer. Keep the existing authorized Gmail sender separate from the recipient, and set Reply-To to the customer.

**Why:** The user wants customers to receive only the owner's response, not emails from two different addresses. They do not have credentials for the owner's receiving inbox.

**How to apply:** Preserve this behavior when changing recipients or revising the contact form. A temporary test recipient must not be treated as the final owner inbox; wait for the user to provide and approve the replacement.
